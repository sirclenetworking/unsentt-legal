Child safety standards.

Unsentt has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. This page explains our standards, how we enforce them, and how to reach our child safety team directly.

Unsentt strictly prohibits child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content, behavior, or user that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, or otherwise harms minors. This applies across every part of our platform — posts, comments, images, profiles, usernames, and private communications — with no exceptions.

01

Our policy

Unsentt does not allow content that depicts, promotes, normalizes, glorifies, or facilitates the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form, real or simulated.
  • Sexualization of minors, including suggestive or exploitative depictions of anyone who is or appears to be under 18.
  • Grooming behavior, including attempts by adults to build inappropriate trust or contact with minors.
  • Solicitation of sexual content, images, or contact involving minors.
  • Sextortion or trafficking of minors, or any content facilitating it.

Accounts found to violate this policy are suspended, and the underlying content is removed. We comply with all applicable child safety laws and regulations in every market where Unsentt is available.

02

How we enforce it

Our approach combines automated content moderation with in-app reporting and an account-level strike system.

🤖
Automated moderation
Every post, comment, and image is automatically screened before it's published, using multiple moderation systems to catch harmful and exploitative content.
🚩
In-app reporting
Every post, comment, and profile on Unsentt can be reported directly from within the app, at any time.
⚠️
Strike system
Violations are logged against the account. Accounts that accumulate repeated violations are automatically suspended.
⛔
Removal & suspension
Content that violates this policy is blocked or removed, and the responsible account is suspended.
03

How to report a concern

If you see something on Unsentt that concerns you, please report it right away using either method below.

  1. In the app: Open the post, comment, or profile in question, tap the menu (⋯) icon, and select Report. Choose the reason that best describes the issue and submit — our moderation team reviews every report.
  2. By email: If it's urgent, or you'd rather not use the in-app flow, email us directly at sirclenetworking@cocial.in. Please include as much detail as possible — usernames, post links, and screenshots if available — so we can act quickly.

If a child is in immediate danger, please contact local law enforcement or emergency services first, in addition to reporting to us.

04

Our team is responsible for handling reports related to CSAM and CSAE, and can speak directly to our prevention practices and compliance on request.
