Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 May 2025  ·  Last updated: 1 May 2025

Unsentt is built around anonymity. Your real name and email address are never stored, never shown publicly, and never sold. This policy explains exactly what we do collect, why, and how you stay in control.

01

Who we are

Unsentt is operated by Sircle Networking Pvt Ltd, a company incorporated in India. References to "we", "us", or "our" in this policy refer to Sircle Networking Pvt Ltd.

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, reach us at support@cocial.in.

02

What we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to run the app safely and well.

Data Why we collect it Shared?
Firebase UID Unique identifier for your account. Never visible to other users. No
Username Your chosen public handle (e.g. @sunflower42). Shown on your posts. Within app only
College / institution Used to filter and display your college feed. Shown on posts. Within app only
Posts & interactions Content you create, reactions, comments, reposts, and reports. Within app only
View duration How long you spend on each post. Used to rank content quality. No
FCM device token Your device's push notification address. Used to send notifications. No
Media (images / GIFs) Files you attach to posts. Stored on Azure Blob Storage. Within app only

We use Google / Apple sign-in for authentication only. Your real name and email address from those providers are never stored in our database and never shown to anyone.

03

How we use your data

We use the data we collect exclusively to:

Run and improve the app Rank content by quality Send push notifications Moderate harmful content Respond to safety reports Detect and prevent abuse

Post text is processed by OpenAI's moderation API to detect policy violations before publishing. OpenAI processes this text solely for moderation and does not use it to train models. No other third party receives your data for advertising or analytics purposes.

04

Third-party services

We use the following services to operate Unsentt:

Service Purpose
Firebase (Google) Authentication, database (Firestore), and cloud functions
Firebase Cloud Messaging Push notifications
OpenAI Content moderation — post text is checked before publishing
Azure Blob Storage (Microsoft) Storage for images and GIFs attached to posts
Apple / Google Sign-In Authentication only — we never receive or store your password

Each service is bound by its own privacy policy. We only share data with them to the extent necessary for them to provide their service to us.

05

Posts and anonymity

Posts on Unsentt are tied to your username, not your real identity. Other users see only your username, avatar, and college name — never your name, email, or any information from your Google or Apple account.

Posts expire automatically 24 hours after creation and are permanently deleted from our database. Reactions, comments, and reposts associated with an expired post are also removed.

Anonymity does not mean immunity. In cases of serious legal violations or safety threats, we may be required by law to disclose account information to authorities.

06

Your rights and controls

You are in control of your data. At any time you can:

Delete individual posts Delete your account from Settings Block other users Report content Request a data export

Deleting your account removes all your posts, comments, reactions, reposts, follow relationships, and your user record from our database. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

To request a copy of your data or ask any questions about your rights, email us at support@cocial.in.

07

Children and age requirements

Unsentt is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we become aware that a user under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete that account and its data immediately.

If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at support@cocial.in and we will investigate promptly.

08

Data retention

Active posts are deleted after 24 hours. Account data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Anonymised, aggregated analytics data (such as total post counts per hashtag) may be retained indefinitely as it cannot be linked to any individual.

09

Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS), Firebase security rules that restrict data access to authenticated users, and server-side validation on all write operations via Cloud Functions.

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please disclose it responsibly by emailing support@cocial.in.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be notified in-app. Continued use of Unsentt after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
