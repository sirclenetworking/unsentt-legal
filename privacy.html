Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Unsentt ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Sircle Networking. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and your rights over it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Authentication data. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive a unique identifier and email address from those providers for authentication purposes only. Your real name and email are never displayed publicly.
  • Account data. Username, avatar, declared age (numeric), gender (optional), college affiliation, and user type.
  • Content you create. Posts, comments, reposts, and reactions you submit.
  • Usage data. How long you view posts, which posts you interact with, and which topics you follow — used to rank your feed.
  • Device data. FCM push-notification token (to deliver notifications), device platform (iOS/Android).
  • Reports. Content you flag as violating our guidelines.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and personalise the Unsentt feed.
  • To enforce our Community Guidelines and keep the platform safe.
  • To send push notifications (reactions, comments, followers, milestones).
  • To award and track the in-app coin reward system.
  • To analyse aggregate usage patterns for product improvements.

3. Content Moderation — Third-Party AI Processing

To keep Unsentt safe, every post and image you submit is automatically reviewed by third-party AI services before it is published. By submitting content you consent to this processing.

  • OpenAI Moderation API — post text is sent to OpenAI's moderation endpoint to detect harmful content categories (violence, hate speech, self-harm, etc.). OpenAI's privacy policy applies: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.
  • OpenAI GPT-4o-mini — borderline posts are further reviewed by a GPT-4o-mini classifier prompt. Same data-processing terms apply.
  • Microsoft Azure AI Content Safety — images and borderline text are sent to Azure Content Safety for category severity scoring. Microsoft's privacy policy applies: privacy.microsoft.com.

We do not use your content to train AI models and we do not sell your content to any party.

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — aggregate usage events. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • PostHog — product analytics (feature usage, funnels). PostHog's privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy.
  • Amplitude — behavioural analytics. Amplitude's privacy policy: amplitude.com/privacy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • The third-party services listed in sections 3 and 4, solely to operate the app.
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your user profile, all posts, comments, reactions, follows, saves, and associated data. Deletion is irreversible and processed immediately.

7. Children's Privacy

Unsentt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at support@cocial.in and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. Contact us at support@cocial.in to exercise these rights.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes via the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
